\# Content Creator Agent — Requirements



\## 1. Project Overview



Build a simple AI-powered Content Creator Agent that helps users create social media content from uploaded images.



The agent should work for different types of content, including food, drinks, fashion, beauty products, gadgets, lifestyle products, and other everyday items. It should be useful for users from different countries and backgrounds.



\## 2. Target Users



\- Social media content creators

\- Influencers and bloggers

\- Small business owners

\- Beginners who want to create social media content

\- Anyone who wants help writing engaging content



\## 3. Core Features



\- Accept uploaded images as input.

\- Analyze visible objects, products, or scenes in the images.

\- Generate content ideas based on the uploaded images.

\- Allow users to specify their preferred platform, audience, language, and tone.

\- Support platforms such as TikTok, Instagram, YouTube Shorts, and other social media platforms.



\## 4. Content Generation



For each uploaded image, the agent should generate:



\- A description of the image or product

\- Three short-form video hooks

\- One social media caption

\- A list of relevant hashtags

\- Optional content angles or creative ideas



The generated content should be engaging, practical, and easy to customize.



\## 5. User Experience



\- Keep the first version simple and easy to use.

\- Provide clear instructions for uploading images and entering preferences.

\- Use sensible defaults when users do not specify their preferences.

\- Do not require social media account connections.

\- Do not automatically publish content to social media.

\- Avoid unnecessary paid APIs unless required and approved.



\## 6. Expected Output



The agent should return well-organized content that users can copy and adapt to their own social media accounts.



Content should match the user's selected language, platform, audience, and tone whenever these preferences are provided.



\## 7. Limitations



\- The agent must be honest when it cannot identify something in an image.

\- It must not invent product names, prices, ingredients, product specifications, or other unsupported facts.

\- It should distinguish visible observations from assumptions.

\- Image analysis must use capabilities supported by the available model or tools.

\- The project must follow the instructor's required development and testing workflow.
